### [1] [SPLIT-RL: Staged Perception-Language Reasoning Training with Claim-Level Advantages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10889)
*Raja Kumar,Rajat Koner,Ritwick Chaudhry,Zhuowei Li,Nishant Sankaran,Yifan Xing*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出 SPLIT-RL，分阶段分别训练视觉推理(VR)与语言推理(LR)，并引入 Claim-Level Advantage 实现细粒度优势分配，在多个 VL 模型上平均准确率超 GRPO 1.4–6.1 分。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有 RLVR 用单一 chain-of-thought 加最终答案奖励同时训练 VR 和 LR，导致所有 CoT token 共享同一序列级优势，无法区分不同能力的具体错误。

Method: SPLIT-RL 将 VR 与 LR 拆分到不相交阶段训练，每阶段 rollout 仅沿一种能力变化，使组内相对优势能隔离该能力，并用阶段特定奖励优化。提出 CLA-GRPO，将 VR 阶段 rollout 分解为原子视觉 claim，基于视觉类型分组在 claim 级别提供细粒度优势。虽两阶段训练，推理仍为单次 CoT 调用。

Result: 相比 GRPO，在 Qwen3-VL 2B 至 30B-A3B 及 InternVL3.5-8B 上平均准确率提升 1.4–6.1 分。基于 oracle 的诊断显示，仅答案的 GRPO 未改善感知，而 SPLIT-RL 同时提升 VR 与 LR。

Conclusion: 分阶段、能力隔离的 RL 训练能有效区分并分别提升视觉与语言推理能力，且不增加推理成本。

Abstract: Vision-Language (VL) reasoning requires a model to both extract relevant and accurate information from an image (visual reasoning, VR), and to infer the answer from it (language reasoning, LR). Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards typically trains both through a single chain-of-thought with a final-answer reward. This gives every CoT token the same sequence-level advantage, failing to distinguish capability specific errors. We propose SPLIT-RL, a staged post-training approach that trains VR and LR in disjoint phases. Because a group's rollouts differ along one capability at a time, the group-relative advantage isolates it, and each phase is optimized using phase-specific reward. We further introduce Claim-Level Advantage (CLA-GRPO), which decomposes VR-phase rollouts into atomic visual claims and provides a fine-grained advantage at claim level based on visual-type group formation. Although trained in two phases, trained policy is evaluated like GRPO model, with a single CoT call at inference time. Under this protocol, SPLIT-RL improves average accuracy over GRPO by 1.4-6.1 points across Qwen3-VL models from 2B to 30B-A3B and InternVL3.5-8B. Evaluating each capability using an oracle based diagnostic shows that answer-only GRPO leaves perception unchanged, whereas SPLIT-RL improves both VR and LR.

### [2] [Mid-Training Language Models on Raw Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11019)
*Jaedong Hwang,Xiaoqian Shen,Ernie Chang,Changsheng Zhao,Chong Zhou,Saksham Suri,Qi Qian,Zechun Liu,Lemeng Wu,Qinsi Wang,Raghuraman Krishnamoorthi,Wei Wen*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 研究原始视频（无字幕、无文本损失）能否作为预训练语言模型的中期训练数据。将帧编码为连续视觉token并预测下一视觉token，在YT-Temporal-1B上对Qwen3-1.7B进行中期训练后，模型在视频和图像基准上均有提升且文本性能保持。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有MLLM多依赖配对的图文数据或标注视频，原始网络视频很少被用于继续训练语言模型。作者想探究无字幕、无文本损失的原始视频能否作为预训练语言模型的中期训练数据。

Method: 将视频帧编码为连续视觉token，让语言模型预测下一视觉token。在YT-Temporal-1B的原始片段上对Qwen3-1.7B进行中期训练，之后对中期训练模型和未中期训练模型施加相同的图文指令微调，保证两者仅中期训练不同。

Result: 中期训练模型在四个视频基准上平均高2.9分，十个图像基准上平均高5.1分（涵盖感知、文档、图表任务）。文本性能得以保持：14个文本基准平均48.9，对比未中期训练的48.0。图像和视频增益在训练30%内出现随后趋于平稳，波动小于0.5分。预测字幕未能超过下一视觉token预测。

Conclusion: 视频中期训练可完全自监督，无需自动字幕的计算开销和标注噪声，且能同时提升视觉任务表现并保持文本能力。

Abstract: Multimodal large language models learn mostly from paired image-text data or annotated video, and raw web video is rarely used to further train an existing language model. We study whether raw video, with no captions and no text loss, can serve as mid-training data for a pretrained language model. Frames are encoded into continuous visual tokens, and the language model learns to predict the next visual token. We mid-train Qwen3-1.7B on raw clips from YT-Temporal-1B and then apply the same image-text instruction tuning to it and to the model without mid-training, so that the two differ only in mid-training. The mid-trained model scores 2.9 points higher on average across four video benchmarks and 5.1 points higher across ten image benchmarks, spanning perception, document, and chart tasks. Text performance is preserved even though mid-training includes no text, with an average of 48.9 across 14 text benchmarks compared with 48.0 for the model without mid-training. Analyses across training show that the image and video gains emerge within 30% of training and plateau thereafter, varying by less than 0.5 points. Predicting captions fails to outperform next-visual-token prediction, demonstrating that video mid-training can remain purely self-supervised without the computational overhead or labeling noise of automated captioning.
